=== NOTES (skipped / adapted metrics) ===
1. Density is reported both in the standard form (m/(n(n-1)) for a directed graph) and in the bipartite form; the standard form is tiny by construction.
2. In a user->URL graph users have in-degree 0 and URLs have out-degree 0, so out-degree is summarised over users only and in-degree over URLs only.
3. Strongly connected components are trivial in a user->URL graph (no directed cycles), so every node is its own SCC; weak components are the meaningful ones.
4. Standard clustering coefficient is 0 for a bipartite graph; the Latapy bipartite clustering coefficient is reported instead, on a random node sample for speed (--samples).
5. Diameter is a lower bound (sampled eccentricities) because the largest component exceeds --exact-limit.
6. Diameter is computed on the undirected version of the largest weakly connected component; the directed diameter of a user->URL graph is trivially 1 (paths have length 1) and most pairs are unreachable.
7. Closeness was computed only for the highest-degree nodes of the largest component (exact all-pairs closeness is too expensive at this size).
8. Betweenness was approximated by only counting shortest paths of length <= 4 (--bc-cutoff).
9. Closeness, betweenness and eigenvector centrality are computed on the undirected view of the largest component; degree centrality and PageRank use the full graph.
